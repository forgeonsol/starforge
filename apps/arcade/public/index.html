A run

Three movements, until the hull gives out.

Chain kills for a score multiplier, skim bullets to charge the forge blast, and dash straight through anything small.

01Swarm

Hold the line

Shard drones swarm, Rams lock a lane and lunge, Leeches drain your heat when they get close.

02Bell

Choose an upgrade

At the bell you take one of three: another barrel, a hotter trigger, piercing rounds, a wing drone or more hull.

03Boss

Break the boss

Every charge is telegraphed. Below half health a boss turns red and changes its pattern.

The three

Bosses of the forge.

Each one has a tell. Learn it and you will always find room to move.

I

Anvil Ray

Wide volleys, then a spinning ring of bolts with a single gap.Tell · wings flare

II

Twin Hammer

Locks a lane, flashes, and charges straight down it.Tell · lane flash

III

Bellows

Breathes out Leech swarms that sap your heat.Tell · plates open
